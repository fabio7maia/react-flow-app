`effect(fn)` runs a side effect for an action without navigating. Use it for what the screen reports on the way (saving a draft), and
`useFlowEvents` for what has to react to navigation itself (dropping the draft on `flow:exit`).

Render `DiscardDraftOnExit` as a child of the `FlowProvider`.
