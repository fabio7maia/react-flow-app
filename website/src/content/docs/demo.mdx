The example store of the repository, running on the library itself, in a simulated browser. Browse it and watch, next to it:

- the **map of the flows**, drawn from the graph, with the current step and the transitions walked;
- the **events** of each navigation, grouped by `navigationId`;
- what the **Segment adapter** sends;
- the **generated tests** and their coverage, each one replayable on screen;
- a **PR review**: the graph diff of a change, as `react-flow-app check --base` reports it;
- the **source** of the current step.
